<h2 align="center">Cozy farming meets advanced industry and industry augmented by the arcane</h2>

Featuring the coziness and survivalist aspects of TerraFirmaCraft, the advanced technological industry of GregTech, with a thorough dash of imaginative realism and magic. This pack is intended to be taken as a marathon, rather than a sprint. There may be friction and aspects that feel grindy, but it is my hope that each component will feel satisfying enough to be a worthwhile investment of your time and energy.

Certain recipes and components are made easier by investing in magitech or more advanced industrial machinery. Developing both in tandem is incentivized, but full-on investment into both is not required.

Exploration is encouraged via the use of climate-dependent resources, mobs, and structures, presenting a worthwhile reason to take to the skies in Aeronautics airships.

What else lies outside this plane of the overworld? Caves deep beneath the surface, otherworldly dimensions beyond the fade, or greater frontiers beyond the stars.
